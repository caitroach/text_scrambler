# text_scrambler
You may have heard that Anthropic is introducing undetectable "watermarks" into all outputs, at the model level. The watermark is invisible, does not change meaning or quality, and can survive copy-pasting, editing, reformatting, and even re-typing. This is not, as I originally thought, due to the insertion of invisible Unicode characters ([which are really easy to find with free online tools](https://www.soscisurvey.de/tools/view-chars.php)) into output text.

So how does it work?

LLMs generate random text with per-token probabilities, which can be generated by random numbers. Watermarking works by subtly altering the probability distribution of each word st some words become more/less likely. To check for an AI watermark, you can do statistical analysis on a given text and see how close it is to the watermark distribution vs the natural distribution. 

That means watermarking is much more reliable for long passages of text than it is for a tweet-length sentence.
